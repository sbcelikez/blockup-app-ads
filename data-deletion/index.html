Data Deletion

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This page explains how to delete data associated with apps published by Tradefleet LLC (“Tradefleet Games”), including BlockUp: Crust.

There is no account to delete

Our apps do not have accounts. You never give us a name, an email address or a password, so there is no profile of you to remove. What exists is a small amount of technical data, described below, and you can clear all of it yourself.

What data exists, and how to delete it

Data How to delete it
Game progress — scores, level, coins, achievements, settings Stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it immediately and permanently.
Analytics and crash reports, linked to an app installation identifier Uninstalling stops all further collection. Existing records are deleted automatically after the retention periods below, or on request.
Advertising data, linked to your device's advertising identifier Reset the identifier in your device settings; this breaks the link to any earlier advertising data.

To reset the advertising identifier: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID on Android, or Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS, where turning off tracking has the same effect.

Asking us to delete it

If you would rather we delete the records on our side, write to info@tradefleet.co with the subject line Data deletion and tell us:

  • which app you use;
  • roughly when you installed it, and on what kind of device.

Because we hold no account data, we cannot look you up by name or email. Those details are what let us locate the right installation records. We reply within 30 days, and we tell you what was deleted.

If we cannot identify your records from the information given — which can happen, since the identifiers are deliberately not tied to you — we will say so plainly rather than delete someone else's data.

What is deleted, and what is kept

A deletion request covers the analytics events, crash reports and advertising records associated with your installation. Nothing is kept afterwards, with one exception: aggregate statistics that contain no identifiers at all — for example, the total number of games played across all players — cannot be traced back to an installation and are not affected.

Automatic deletion

Even without a request, data is deleted on a schedule: analytics records after 14 months, crash reports after 90 days. Advertising data is governed by Google's own retention policies.
